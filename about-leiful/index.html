About Leiful

Science, and a person who knows you.

I studied health science and came out frustrated: so much of modern health care treats symptoms, not people.

I've coached one-on-one for more than ten years and worked with more than 200 clients. The women I met kept telling the same story. They'd done everything right, tried every app and plan, and still nobody was looking at the whole picture: their body, their week, their life.

Leiful is the program I built for them. Say lay-ful, from the Italian lei, "she".
