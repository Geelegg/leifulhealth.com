About Guy

Science, and a person who knows you.

I studied health science and came out frustrated: so much of modern health care treats symptoms, not people. I've coached one-on-one since the mid-2010s, online since 2018, and worked with more than 200 clients.

The women I met kept telling the same story. They'd done everything right, tried every app and plan, and still nobody was looking at the whole picture: their body after 40, their week, their life. Leiful is the program I built for them.

B. Sciences (Exercise & Nutrition) · Accredited Sports Nutritionist · Personal Trainer · Workshops at Brisbane studios
