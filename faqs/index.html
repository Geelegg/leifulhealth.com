FAQs

Questions, answered.

Ask something else
What does it cost?

Your Roadmap call covers it. You can pay upfront or through Zip.

What's the Personal Health Roadmap?

45 minutes with Guy, $100, credited to your Season if you join. You leave with a clear picture of where you are and what's possible.

Do I have to give up wine, eating out or holidays?

No. They're built in.

I already do Pilates. Will I have to stop?

No. We build around it, and help you get more from it.

Is this just a meal plan?

No. A meal plan is what an app gives you. This is six months with an expert who knows your body, your numbers and your week.

How much time does it take?

About two hours a week of your attention, plus two minutes a day for your check-in.

What if I'm not in Brisbane?

Everything works online. Your Kick-off is in person in Brisbane, or by video if you're further away.

What happens after 26 weeks?

Graduation at a gathering, with your Season Report. Then the Club, if you want it.

Is it safe with my health condition or medication?

Everyone is screened first. Some answers need your GP's OK before we start, and we work with your GP where needed.

Is this weight loss?

It's health first. How you feel, your energy and your strength lead. Many women lose weight along the way, and we measure plenty of other things too.
