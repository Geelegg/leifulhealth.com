Your Season

Your health has changed. The way you look after it should too.

Most women end up managing it alone, fitting it in around work, family and everything else, with advice that was written for someone else.

Our Method

Health, done properly.

13 consults across your 26 weeks, more often at the start, beginning with an in-person Kick-off. Support between consults when something can't wait.

Meal plans, recipes and targets built for your body, your tastes and your week, updated whenever you need a change. Built to current health guidelines.

More from what you already do, whether that's Pilates, walks or the gym, with your own guide to getting the most from it. A full program if you'd like one.

Your vision, written with Guy and revisited halfway and at the end. Daily and weekly check-ins, two minutes each. Guy reads every one.

Small changes, added one or two at a time through your Season, so they're still there long after it.

Your plan, weigh-ins and trends, check-ins, reports and guides, all in the Leiful app. Your files are yours for life.

Meet Guy

An expert who knows your name.

Guy Leggett has coached one-on-one for more than ten years and worked with more than 200 clients. He holds a degree in exercise and nutrition and is an accredited sports nutritionist.

He built Leiful for the women he kept meeting who'd done everything right and still weren't being looked after.
