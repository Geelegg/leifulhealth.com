Your Leiful Season.

26 weeks. One-on-one. Built around your life.

The idea

Your health has changed, and the way you look after it should too. Most women end up managing it alone, fitting it in around work, family and everything else, with advice that was written for someone else.

Your Season changes that. For six months, you have an expert in your corner who knows your body, your numbers and your week. Together you build your nutrition, movement and habits around the life you already have, and adjust them as you go, until feeling strong and energised is simply how you live.

Nutrition. Movement. Mindset. Data. Guy.

What's in your Season

Every piece, in one plan.

Guy, in your corner

  • 13 one-on-one consults, more often at the start
  • An in-person Kick-off and your printed Season pack
  • Advice on nutrition, movement and supplements, with no products to sell you
  • Monthly progress reports
  • Support between consults

Nutrition

  • Meal plans, recipes and targets built for your body and your week
  • Updated whenever you need a change
  • Up to 60 micronutrients tracked
  • Recipes and a shopping list included

Movement

  • More from what you already do: Pilates, walks, the gym
  • Your own guide to getting the most from your training
  • A full program if you'd like one

Mindset

  • Your vision, written with Guy and revisited halfway and at the end
  • Daily and weekly check-ins, two minutes each, read by Guy

Habits

  • One or two at a time, building through your Season

Everything in one place

  • The Leiful app: plan, weigh-ins, trends, check-ins and reports
  • The Method, your course and guides, in the app and in print
  • Quarterly gatherings with other women in their Season
Graduation
You graduate, and we celebrate it properly. At the next gathering, you'll receive your Season Report: where you started, where you finished, what you learned, and your plan for the next season of your life.
Plus a few things made just for you, and an invitation to the Leiful Club.

Is it for you?

It's for you if
  • You want to feel strong, energised and at home in your body again
  • You've done the reading and want it built into your real life
  • You're ready to give it six months
It isn't
  • A gym, PT or Pilates replacement
  • A diet: nourished, not deprived
  • An app with a chatbot: it's a real person
