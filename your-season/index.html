Your Leiful Season

26 weeks. One-on-one. Built around your life.

Six months with Guy: a plan for your body, your week and the life you're keeping. Then the know-how to keep it for good.

The shape of the 26 weeks

How your Season runs

  • Week 0: welcome pack and at-home photos.
  • Week 1: orientation.
  • Weeks 1–2: in-person Kick-off, including how to get more out of your Pilates.
  • Then 14 consults: two in week 1, weekly to week 4, fortnightly to week 14, then monthly, with a final push at weeks 22–26.
  • Graduation: a recorded final session, your cookbook and your Club card.

Investment

A premium, fully one-on-one program.

Places are limited each Season. Apply, and we'll talk through it on a free call. Pay upfront or through Zip.

Who it's for
  • Professional women, 40+ at the core, who've tried everything.
  • Women who want a plan that fits their life, not a life that fits a plan.
  • Not for crash diets, quick fixes or challenges.

Everyone is screened first. Some health conditions need your GP's OK before we start.
